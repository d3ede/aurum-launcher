# Aurum Launcher 0.6.5

- Minecraft can automatically join an invited world using its native LAN connector. Vanilla 26.3 and Fabric 1.21.11 were checked with actual game clients; older unsupported clients retain LAN-list fallback.
- Social reconnects after isolated-worker failures without creating another identity or dropping friends. Missing identity files require restoration rather than silently replacing the ID.
- Redesigned invitation cards, aligned profile actions, an animated continuous Aurum ID and a shorter QR-code heading.
- Pending manual downloads stay available inside their instance and no longer open a dialog every time the launcher starts.
- Includes grouped light/dark themes, immediate theme saving, two light palettes and the revised Ukrainian translation.

Social remains experimental: completed Minecraft gameplay between two computers on separate networks has not been verified. Local integration checks are not evidence of Internet NAT traversal.

Windows x64 installer, signed updater manifest and SHA-256 checksums are prepared locally. Existing instances, accounts and settings are retained.
