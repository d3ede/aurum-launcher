# Brand assets

`aurum-gold.svg` is the approved Aurum logo exported from the Launcher Figma file.

The service logos identify third-party catalog sources; they retain their original geometry and brand colors:

- `modrinth.svg`: [Modrinth's official SVG](https://github.com/modrinth/code/blob/main/apps/frontend/src/assets/images/logo.svg), with its brand color `#1BD96A` resolved for standalone SVG rendering. Modrinth branding remains the property of Rinth, Inc.; see [the upstream notice](https://github.com/modrinth/code/blob/main/apps/frontend/COPYING.md).
- `curseforge.svg`: [CurseForge's original mark in Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/curseforge.svg), rendered in its brand color `#F16436`. Simple Icons is [CC0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md); CurseForge's trademark remains the property of its owner.
- `atlauncher.svg`: the unmodified standalone vector from [ATLauncher's official branding resources](https://atlauncher.com/branding), downloaded from [logo.svg](https://atlauncher.com/assets/images/logo.svg). Its original cube geometry, colors and transparent background are retained.
- `ftb.svg`: the unmodified colored FTB mark used by the [official FTB website](https://www.feed-the-beast.com/), from its [ftb-logo-no-footing SVG asset](https://www.feed-the-beast.com/_next/static/media/ftb-logo-no-footing.0v-8ku-83u2_k.svg). The canvas aspect ratio and original colors are preserved; internal CSS padding keeps the complete mark within the shared icon slot. FTB branding remains the property of Feed The Beast Ltd.
- `technic.png`: the unmodified icon from [Technic's official launcher resources](https://github.com/TechnicPack/LauncherV3/blob/master/src/main/resources/icon.png). The original blue emblem is used at menu size, without redrawing or recoloring it. Technic branding remains the property of its owner. The launcher repository's [license](https://github.com/TechnicPack/LauncherV3/blob/master/LICENSE) covers its original materials; trademarks are not relicensed by Aurum.

These assets do not imply affiliation or endorsement.
