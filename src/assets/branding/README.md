# Brand assets

`aurum-gold.svg` is the approved Aurum logo exported from the Launcher Figma file.

The service logos identify third-party catalog sources; they retain their original geometry and brand colors:

- `modrinth.svg`: [Modrinth's official SVG](https://github.com/modrinth/code/blob/main/apps/frontend/src/assets/images/logo.svg), with its brand color `#1BD96A` resolved for standalone SVG rendering. Modrinth branding remains the property of Rinth, Inc.; see [the upstream notice](https://github.com/modrinth/code/blob/main/apps/frontend/COPYING.md).
- `curseforge.svg`: [CurseForge's original mark in Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/curseforge.svg), rendered in its brand color `#F16436`. Simple Icons is [CC0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md); CurseForge's trademark remains the property of its owner.

These assets do not imply affiliation or endorsement.
